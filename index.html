Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there parking available?

Yes, we provide ample free parking space for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers exclusively for our members and their guests.

Can I bring guests for meetings?

Absolutely. You can book our meeting cabins for private discussions. Guests are welcome in the lounge areas for brief catch-ups as well.

What are your operating hours?

Our workspace is accessible 24/7 for Dedicated Desk and Private Cabin members via biometric entry. Hot desk members have access during standard business hours (8 AM - 8 PM).

Is there a power backup?

Yes, we have a robust industrial-grade generator backup ensuring uninterrupted power supply and high-speed internet even during grid outages.
