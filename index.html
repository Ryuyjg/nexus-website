FAQ & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about access, utility billing, and rules at Lumina Kuttiady.

When can I access my workspace?

+

Dedicated Desk and Private Cabin members have 24/7 biometric building access. Hot Desk members can access the workspace during business hours (8:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Monday to Saturday).

Are there utility charges or electricity bills?

+

No. All utility costs, including premium air conditioning, backup power, water, and cleaning services are 100% included in your monthly membership fee.

Are meeting room credits included?

+

Yes. Hot Desk members receive 2 hours of meeting room bookings per month. Dedicated Desks get 5 hours, and Private Cabins receive 15+ hours. Additional credits can be purchased at discounted rates.

Is there a lock-in period or contract?

+

No, we keep it simple. All plans run on flexible month-to-month terms. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription by giving a 15-day notice before your next renewal.

Can I receive packages and mail?

+

Yes. Private Cabin and Dedicated Desk plans include mail handling and receptionist sign-off. We notify you via WhatsApp/Email as soon as your package arrives.
